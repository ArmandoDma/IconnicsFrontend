Frequently Asked Questions

How does the app calculate my hydration?

The app tracks your logged water intake and compares it with your recommended daily hydration based on weight, activity level and environment.

Why is my heart rate showing 0 bpm?

This usually means your wearable device isn’t synced. Go to Settings → Devices and reconnect your wearable sensor.

Where can I find my activity history?

You can see your full activity log in the Performance tab. There you’ll find graphs, daily summaries, and weekly insights.

How do benefits and discounts work?

Benefits unlock automatically when you complete activity goals. These include gym discounts, wellness coupons and more.
